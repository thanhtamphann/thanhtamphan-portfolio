A strong hook is more than the first sentence. It gives the viewer a clear reason to continue and sets up a question the video can answer.

## Start with a promise

The opening should create useful curiosity without giving everything away at once.

## Keep renewing curiosity

Each section should add a new detail, contrast or question so the viewer has another reason to stay.
